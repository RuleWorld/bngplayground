# Patterns, bonds, and states

Components may have explicit state values and bond labels. `A(b!1).B(a!1)` is a connected complex; `A(b).B(a)` is a disconnected pattern. A `+` in a reaction separates reactant/product molecularity, while `.` connects molecules within one pattern. Avoid inventing undeclared sites or states and check whether a rule requires a bond to be present or absent.
