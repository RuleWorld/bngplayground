## 2026-07-24 - ARIA labels for Toggle Buttons
**Learning:** In complex configuration panels like ExpressionInputPanel, parameter estimation, and timeline controls, icon-only buttons or state-dependent toggles frequently lack adequate semantic context. Adding explicit ARIA labels that describe the specific mode or action improves accessibility significantly without altering the visual footprint.
**Action:** Proactively review any new or modified toggle groups and icon-based action buttons to ensure they have descriptive ARIA labels, especially those dictating application state.
