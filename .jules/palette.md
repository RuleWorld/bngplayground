## 2024-07-26 - Add ARIA labels to toggle and suggestion buttons
**Learning:** Adding descriptive ARIA labels to toggle buttons (like view modes or math/pattern modes) significantly improves the experience for screen reader users by providing clear context, especially when visual cues like active states are used.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only buttons or buttons grouped for toggling modes have explicit, descriptive `aria-label` attributes to maintain accessibility standards.
